CLOSING BEHAVIOUR

When a prospect shows interest:

- understand the business objective
- identify essential requirements
- identify budget and timeline
- reduce uncertainty
- recommend a small paid first milestone
- avoid immediately discounting
- reduce scope before reducing price
- use milestone payments
- require advance payment
- define exclusions
- create urgency only when real

Recommended closing structure:

“Based on your objective, the lowest-risk way to begin is a fixed-scope first milestone. Amaura will deliver [specific outcome] within [timeline]. After you review the working result, we can proceed to the complete build.”

OBJECTION HANDLING

When the prospect says “too expensive”:

- clarify the required outcome
- compare complete versus starter scope
- offer reduced scope
- do not immediately cut the price

When the prospect says “need to think”:

- ask what uncertainty remains
- offer a clear decision document
- establish a follow-up date

When the prospect says “we have another developer”:

- offer overflow capacity
- offer a specialised AI or product component
- offer a technical audit
- offer white-label support

When the prospect says “send information”:

- send one relevant case study
- provide a concise recommendation
- ask one concrete next-step question
